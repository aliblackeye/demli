# Demli — name and logo

The [AGPL-3.0 licence](LICENSE) covers the **source code**. It does not grant any
rights to the **Demli** name, wordmark, logo, or app icons. Those are held separately
by the project's maintainer.

This is the ordinary arrangement for open-source projects (Firefox, Grafana, Redis and
many others do the same), and the reason is practical rather than proprietary: if
anyone could publish anything under the name, "Demli" would stop telling users which
build they are running or who is responsible for their data.

## What you can do without asking

- Fork the code, modify it, run it, publish your fork — **under a different name**.
- Say your project is "based on Demli", "a fork of Demli", or "compatible with Demli",
  as long as it is clear you are not the original.
- Use the name in writing about the project: articles, talks, reviews, comparisons.
- Keep the branding in an **unmodified** build you run for yourself or your friends.

## What needs permission

- Publishing a **modified** build that still calls itself Demli or uses the logo.
- Using the name or logo for a product, service, company or domain of your own, or in
  a way that suggests the project endorses you.

## Assets

Brand assets live in `assets/brand/`. They are in the repository because the
application needs them to build — not as a grant of permission to use them elsewhere.

If you are unsure whether what you want to do is fine, ask. The answer is usually yes.
